Expected Excel Format

The system expects the GECII Feeder Status Excel format with the following column groups:

General Details
SN, Region, Circle, T&C, ESD, Substation Name, GPS, Type
GSS Connectivity
Primary GSS, Alternate GSS, Tapping info, LILO info
Transformer Details
Capacity (MVA), Make, YOM, Max Loading, OTI, WTI
Feeder Details
Feeder Name, Voltage (33kV/11kV)
Meter Details
Meter No., Make, Type, Status, CTR, MF, CT type/status
Switchgear & Protection
VCB type/make/YOM, Relay type/make, Status, Year commissioned

⚠ Data starts at row 4 (rows 1–3 are merged headers). Multiple sheets are supported. Re-uploading for an existing substation will overwrite all its feeder and transformer records.
